HOW THIS WORKS

ABOUT THIS CHART

A scheduled GitHub Action queries the faucet's bridge server every 5 hours for two numbers: the total MEOWFRIEND_TICKET supply actually issued on the Meowcoin chain, and the sum of every player's current in-game ticket balance. The gap between the two lines is roughly how many tickets have been withdrawn out to real wallets rather than still sitting in-game.

⚠ PROOF OF CONCEPT — NO REAL VALUE

MEOWFRIEND_TICKET is a valueless, proof-of-concept Meowcoin asset. This chart exists to make the supply mechanics visible and testable, not to represent anything of monetary value.
