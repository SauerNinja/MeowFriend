ABOUT

WHAT IS MEOWCOIN?

Meowcoin (MEWC) is a proof-of-work cryptocurrency built around one idea: mining that also helps animals. Miners secure the network with MeowPow, an energy-conscious algorithm in the ProgPow family that's friendly to ordinary GPUs, and every block sends a portion of its reward to animal shelters and pet-welfare causes. The project launched in 2022 with a fair distribution — no premine, no ICO, no insider allocation — so new holders start on the same footing as everyone else. MeowFriend exists to make that first step easier: claim a small starter balance, and go explore the network.

FAQ

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What is Meowcoin (MEWC)?
Meowcoin is a cat-themed, proof-of-work cryptocurrency that launched in 2022 with a fair launch — no premine, no ICO. It runs on MeowPow, a GPU-mineable algorithm in the ProgPow family, and sends a share of block rewards to animal shelters.
How much MEWC do I get, and how often?
10 MEWC per boop, once every 24 hours. The RECHARGE screen counts down to your next claim.
Do I need to sign up or connect a wallet?
No. There's no account and nothing to connect. Paste a Meowcoin address that starts with M and boop.
Is MeowFriend affiliated with the official Meowcoin project?
No. MeowFriend is an independent, community-built faucet and isn't affiliated with or endorsed by the Meowcoin core team.
How does the drip actually get paid out?
This build simulates the drip locally in your browser so the full flow can be tested end to end. Wiring it up to a live Meowcoin node and hot wallet is the next step before a public launch.
