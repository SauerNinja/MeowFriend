HOW GOTCHYA WORKS

Your pet's hunger, happiness, and energy drain slowly over real time — even while this tab is closed, so check back and take care of it. Feeding, playing, and letting it rest all cost tickets and restore stats. Neglect it too long and its mood will show it.

⚠ SAVED ONLY IN THIS BROWSER

Your pet, its stats, and its items are stored in this browser's local storage only — clearing site data, switching browsers, or switching devices will lose it. A persistent account tied to a registered wallet is planned for later.
